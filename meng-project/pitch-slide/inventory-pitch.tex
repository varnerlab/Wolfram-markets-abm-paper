\note{The current model lets a trader with no shares continue to send a Sell
signal to its four neighbors. We ask whether that assumption sustains selling
cascades. Students will keep the trader's intention and the corrected reserve
clearing mechanism, but replace its visible Sell signal with Hold whenever it
has zero shares after settlement. The diagrams show the same empty seller
under the two observation rules. Neighboring circles do not show outcomes.
Compare original and inventory-dependent visibility, each with and without
the persistent rule switch, from the same market history. Measure raw
intentions, completed trades, and drawdown separately. Holding technical
traders removes their price-response channel, but inventory-dependent
visibility introduces a new channel from holdings to later intentions.
The hypothesis is reduced spread, but the nonmonotone rule table also allows
amplification. Deliverables are tested Julia code, reproducible experiments,
and a paper in arXiv format. Proposed scope: 3--4 students, 14 weeks.
Sources: paper-arxiv/sections/discussion.tex, explicit visibility limitation;
paper-arxiv/sections/methods.tex, state codes, timing, and paired controls;
code/src/Simulate.jl and code/src/ReserveClearing.jl, current implementation.
Repository inspected September 28, 2026, commit 81cfd17.}
