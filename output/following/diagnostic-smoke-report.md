Control contrasts are paired within starting market and design replicate. Copy-trading masking is compared with fixed replay, so the source tapes stay unchanged. Other controls are compared with live following at the original settings. Size and horizon comparisons are sensitivity analyses; they do not hold the physical population or observation period constant.
