# Inventory-information comparison

The completed study contains 4,400 full financial trajectories over 50 fresh
starting markets: 2,800 initial runs and 1,600 added by the predeclared extension.
Every four-branch comparison shares its pre-intervention history. The baseline
simulation source and archived original study were preserved.

At the primary setting (f=0.20, h=100, technical traders held), the change in
the switch effect on the late raw Sell fraction was -52.28 percentage points,
95% interval [-54.46, -49.86]. Original and inventory-conditioned switched
branches averaged 77.78% and 25.48% in Sell over their final 500 steps; their
controls averaged 24.82% and 24.79%. Majority formation changed from 50/50 to
0/50. This is a finite-sample result, not an impossibility claim.

The mean paired increase in drawdown fell from 23.16 to 2.02 percentage points.
Completed interior sales increased from 490.11 to 748.22 per interior trader.
Thus a smaller raw Sell fraction did not imply fewer completed sales. In the
original switched branch, zero-share senders supplied 39.96% of interior Sell
signals through onset and 78.69% across the observation period, averaging each
market's ratio and counting each sender once per step.

With inventory-conditioned observations, no primary-setting market reached a
majority at f=0.50, but 44/50 reached one at f=0.75, where the late mean Sell
fraction was 31.52%. The observation intervention changed the observed
fraction response rather than establishing elimination of majority formation.
This tests one hypothetical response to inventory disclosure, not the
availability or use of that information in actual markets.

The observer passed 80 tests and the unchanged reserve engine passed its
6,028 existing checks. All 4,400 trajectories passed accounting and pairing
checks. An independent read-only audit reproduced all 160 paired contrasts
and intervals, checked every run summary and all 16 retained histories, and
confirmed the revised claims after a scope correction about financial
feedback with technical traders held.

See Protocol.md and Protocol.json for the fixed design, Extension.json for
its executed extension decisions, Validation.json for accounting and the
primary endpoint, Contrasts.csv and Paired-Effects.csv for inference, and
Branch-Summary.csv and Run-Summary.csv for all conditions. Reporting.json
records the post-processing generators separately from frozen computation.
