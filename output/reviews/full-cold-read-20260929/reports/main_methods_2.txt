methods.P6: Technical traders occupy a random quarter of edge positions and follow a contrarian band rule. They Buy if the quoted price falls below mu_t - k_j sigma_t, Sell if it rises above mu_t + k_j sigma_t, and otherwise Hold. mu_t and sigma_t are an exponentially smoothed mean and absolute deviation (alpha=1/60), and k_j ~ U[0.5,1.5] is drawn per trader.
methods.P7: Gives the order within a step: scheduled changes, orders from existing intentions, settlement, then technical updates followed by interior updates. Defines the "held" condition, in which technical traders stay in Hold from the start so prices cannot affect any intention, and the "active" condition.
methods.P8: Sets out the paired design. The switched market and its no-switch control share a starting market and 1,000 steps under Rule 19280. The switch comes at step 1,001 and 3,000 steps follow. There are 50 starting markets per condition, traders start with 1,000 cash and 10 shares, and a list of robustness checks is deferred to the appendix.
methods.P9: Gives the parameter settings for the fraction, dealer-depth, size and trader-resource campaigns, including the 3x3 cash and share multipliers applied at the switch without no-switch controls.
methods.P10: Describes the fee campaign. Fees of 0-25 bp apply from initialization, with all combinations of f, h and held/active and controls shared across fractions, 8,400 runs in total. Runs are compared against controls and against zero fees, with bootstrap CIs over starting markets.
methods.P11: Defines Sell fraction, majority, onset, final-500-step mean, susceptibility, interpolated f25/f50/f75, maximum drawdown, suppression and rejection fractions, summary statistics and the active-held paired count. Each piece is understandable, but the paragraph is overloaded and mixes in fee-specific and pooling details.
methods.P12: Describes the adaptive refinement of fractions for the size campaign, bootstrap CIs for f25/f50/f75, the width and the maximum susceptibility, and a logistic fit as a sensitivity check. It closes with what the uncertainty estimates do and do not cover.
methods.P13: Explains how example runs were picked for the figures: closest to the median within fraction-by-outcome groups, chosen before viewing images. Gives the settings for the dealer example and the extension of five examples to 10,000 steps.
