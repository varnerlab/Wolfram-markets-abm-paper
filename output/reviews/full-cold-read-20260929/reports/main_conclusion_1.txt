conclusion.P1: A persistent one-response rule change in a minority of interior traders can produce a Sell-intention majority without price feedback. The fraction required shifts with grid size and observation time. How far the price falls depends on trader holdings and on the dealer's constant-product reserves, not on the majority alone. This is mostly clear, but S2 splices two claims together, S5 drops the no-switch-control caveat, and S6's "limited resources" logic is muddled.
