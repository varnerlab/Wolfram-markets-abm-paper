conclusion.P1: Changing the rule of part of the interior traders can produce a Sell majority even when no decision responds to price, and the fraction needed varies with market size and observation time. The size of the price decline is set by trader holdings and dealer reserves, not by the Sell majority alone: shallow dealer reserves bring severe declines, zero depth freezes the price, and fewer trader shares shrink declines. The last few claims are compressed enough that the dealer-versus-trader contrast has to be reconstructed.
