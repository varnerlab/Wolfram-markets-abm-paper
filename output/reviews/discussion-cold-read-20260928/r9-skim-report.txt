discussion.P1: A lasting alteration to selected traders' local responses can spread selling intentions even though other traders retain their rules. The effect differs from a temporary sell instruction and depends on the particular rule alteration. Paired controls isolate the intervention, which represents an imposed behavioral change rather than learning or transmission of a strategy.

discussion.P2: Local responses to neighboring intentions can produce Sell majorities without feedback from prices. Spatial patterns suggest growing groups of sellers, but finite markets and observation periods do not establish an asymptotic threshold or a classical nucleation mechanism.

discussion.P3: The reserve mechanism determines how available resources translate selling into price declines. Small positive dealer reserves can accommodate eligible orders while prices fall sharply; zero reserves instead impose a qualitatively different fixed-price matching condition. Paired controls are essential because shallow-reserve markets can decline sharply without the intervention.

discussion.P5: The contribution relative to established agent-based markets is an imposed, reproducible local-rule intervention whose spread can be studied with and without price feedback while distinguishing intentions from completed trades.
