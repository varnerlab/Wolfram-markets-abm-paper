Plain meaning of discussion.P1: A persistent change to selected traders' local response can produce widespread selling intentions. Its persistence matters, and its effect depends on the specific response changed. Paired markets isolate the intervention's effect at a common starting state, while repeated starting states describe its frequency. The experiment assigns rules rather than explaining how traders learn or adopt them, and selling intentions alone do not determine the price decline.

Plain meaning of discussion.P2: Neighbor interactions can produce a Sell majority even when price changes cannot influence decisions. Spatial clusters and sharper probability changes in larger grids suggest analogies with interacting physical systems, but finite observations do not establish an infinite-system threshold or classical nucleation. The demonstrated mechanism is local propagation over the tested sizes and times.

Plain meaning of discussion.P5: Relative to established agent-based markets, this model sacrifices learning and strategic adaptation to make an assigned local rule change reproducible and systematically examinable. Its contribution is to isolate the spread of selling intentions, test whether that spread requires price feedback, and determine how a particular resource-constrained execution mechanism translates intentions into price declines.
