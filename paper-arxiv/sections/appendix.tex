\subsection{One complete simulation step}

Every campaign used the complete update in Algorithm~\ref{alg:model-step},
with order clearing and settlement defined in
Algorithm~\ref{alg:dealer-clearing}.
Orders at step $t$ came from the lattice $X_{t-1}$ and the boundary states
$e_{i,t-1}$, settled at the standing quote $P_{t-1}$, and generated the new
quote $P_t$. The new quote classified the boundary before the cellular
automaton produced $X_t$. Stating the full order of operations was
necessary because changing that order would define a different model.

\begin{algorithm}[!ht]
\caption{One endogenous-market step, $t-1\rightarrow t$, in simulation order.
Orders came from the old interior and boundary states, settled at the old
quote, and generated the new quote. The new quote set the boundary before the
interior cellular automaton update.}
\label{alg:model-step}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\REQUIRE lattice $X_{t-1}$; boundary states $e_i(t-1)$; trader books; dealer
ledger; quote and band state $(P_{t-1},\mu_{t-1},\sigma_{t-1})$; rule field
$R_i$; parameters; simulation seed
\ENSURE lattice $X_t$; boundary states $e_i(t)$; settled books and dealer;
quote and band state $(P_t,\mu_t,\sigma_t)$; all order counts
\IF{$t$ is the scheduled rule-switch step}
  \STATE $R_i\leftarrow19286$ for every preselected target site $i$
    \COMMENT{preserve every dynamic state variable}
\ENDIF
\IF{$t$ is a scheduled balance-sheet intervention}
  \FORALL{traders $i$}
    \STATE $w_i\leftarrow g_w w_i$; $q_i\leftarrow\operatorname{round}(g_q q_i)$
  \ENDFOR
\ENDIF
\STATE call Algorithm~\ref{alg:dealer-clearing} using states at $t-1$ and
$P_{t-1}$
\STATE receive $(n_t^B,n_t^S,u_t^B,u_t^S,r_t^B,r_t^S)$ and settled ledgers
\STATE $\phi_t\leftarrow(n_t^B-n_t^S)/N_{\rm tr}$
\STATE $P_t\leftarrow P_{t-1}\exp(\lambda\phi_t)$
\STATE $\mu_t\leftarrow\mu_{t-1}+\alpha(P_t-\mu_{t-1})$
\STATE $\sigma_t\leftarrow\sigma_{t-1}+\alpha(|P_t-\mu_t|-\sigma_{t-1})$
\IF{boundary mode is endogenous}
  \FORALL{active boundary traders $i$}
    \STATE $e_i(t)\leftarrow0$ if $P_t<\mu_t-k_i\sigma_t$; $2$ if
    $P_t>\mu_t+k_i\sigma_t$; $1$ otherwise
  \ENDFOR
\ELSE
  \STATE $e_i(t)\leftarrow1$ for every active boundary trader $i$
\ENDIF
\STATE write $e_i(t)$ to active boundary sites; leave passive sites at Hold
\FORALL{strictly interior sites $i$, simultaneously}
  \STATE $c_i(t)\leftarrow\sum_{j\in\partial i}s_j$ using old interior states
  and new boundary states
  \STATE $s_i(t)\leftarrow\mathcal{R}_{R_i}(c_i(t))$
\ENDFOR
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\begin{algorithm}[!ht]
\caption{Order eligibility, dealer clearing, rationing, and settlement at
$P_{t-1}$. The finite dealer filled only the unmatched residual. The
$h=\infty$ branch filled every eligible order.}
\label{alg:dealer-clearing}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\REQUIRE old interior and boundary states; books $(w_i,q_i,\bar c_i)$; dealer
$(W_{t-1},Q_{t-1})$ and bounds $(Q_{\min},Q_{\max},C)$; $P_{t-1}$; $h$;
cost-basis toggle; seed and step $t$
\ENSURE settled books and dealer; executed counts $(n_t^B,n_t^S)$; agent
suppression $(u_t^B,u_t^S)$; dealer rejection $(r_t^B,r_t^S)$
\STATE $\mathcal{B},\mathcal{S}\leftarrow\varnothing$; $u_t^B,u_t^S\leftarrow0$
\FORALL{interior traders, followed by active-boundary traders $i$}
  \STATE $o_i\leftarrow +1$ for Buy, $-1$ for Sell, and $0$ for Hold
    \COMMENT{an edge trader reads its stored boundary state}
  \IF{$o_i=+1$ and $w_i\geq P_{t-1}$}
    \STATE append $i$ to $\mathcal{B}$
  \ELSIF{$o_i=-1$, $q_i\geq1$, and the cost-basis test passes}
    \STATE append $i$ to $\mathcal{S}$
  \ELSIF{$o_i=+1$}
    \STATE $u_t^B\leftarrow u_t^B+1$
  \ELSIF{$o_i=-1$}
    \STATE $u_t^S\leftarrow u_t^S+1$
  \ENDIF
\ENDFOR
\STATE $B_t\leftarrow|\mathcal{B}|$; $S_t\leftarrow|\mathcal{S}|$;
$n_t^B\leftarrow B_t$; $n_t^S\leftarrow S_t$
\IF{$h<\infty$ and $B_t>S_t$}
  \STATE $K_t^B\leftarrow Q_{t-1}-Q_{\min}$
    \COMMENT{shares that the dealer could sell}
  \STATE $n_t^B\leftarrow S_t+\min(B_t-S_t,K_t^B)$
\ELSIF{$h<\infty$ and $S_t>B_t$}
  \STATE $K_t^S\leftarrow Q_{\max}-Q_{t-1}$
    \COMMENT{inventory space}
  \STATE $K_t^S\leftarrow\min\!\left(K_t^S,
  \left\lfloor(W_{t-1}+C)/P_{t-1}\right\rfloor\right)$
    \COMMENT{cash limit; $C=0$ in this study}
  \STATE $n_t^S\leftarrow B_t+\min(S_t-B_t,K_t^S)$
\ENDIF
\STATE $r_t^B\leftarrow B_t-n_t^B$; $r_t^S\leftarrow S_t-n_t^S$
\IF{$r_t^B>0$}
  \STATE sort $\mathcal{B}$ by $z(\mathrm{seed},t,i,+1)$, then by trader identity
\ENDIF
\IF{$r_t^S>0$}
  \STATE sort $\mathcal{S}$ by $z(\mathrm{seed},t,i,-1)$, then by trader identity
\ENDIF
\FORALL{the first $n_t^B$ traders $i$ in $\mathcal{B}$}
  \STATE $\bar c_i\leftarrow(\bar c_iq_i+P_{t-1})/(q_i+1)$;
  $q_i\leftarrow q_i+1$; $w_i\leftarrow w_i-P_{t-1}$
\ENDFOR
\FORALL{the first $n_t^S$ traders $i$ in $\mathcal{S}$}
  \STATE $q_i\leftarrow q_i-1$; $w_i\leftarrow w_i+P_{t-1}$
    \COMMENT{$\bar c_i$ was unchanged on a sale}
\ENDFOR
\STATE $W_t\leftarrow W_{t-1}+P_{t-1}(n_t^B-n_t^S)$
\STATE $Q_t\leftarrow Q_{t-1}+n_t^S-n_t^B$
\RETURN $(n_t^B,n_t^S,u_t^B,u_t^S,r_t^B,r_t^S)$ and settled ledgers
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

The implementation kept two forms of nonexecution separate. Agent suppression
meant that the trader lacked cash or shares before matching. Dealer rejection
meant that the trader was eligible but the finite dealer could not absorb the
unmatched residual. Neither event altered the trader's CA state. This
distinction prevented a financial constraint from silently rewriting the
behavioral state that generated the order.

\subsection{Rationing and paired reproducibility}

When dealer capacity was binding, only the excess side required rationing. Each
eligible trader on that side received a 64-bit score from a SplitMix64
finalizer applied to the tuple
$(\text{seed},t,\text{trader identity},\text{side})$. We sorted orders by
score and used trader identity as a deterministic tie breaker. Priorities changed with $t$,
so a trader who submitted the same order repeatedly did not retain the same
position. Paired paths also used the same score for a given
$(t,i,\text{side})$ after their eligible sets diverged. The score did not
consume draws from the random-number generator used to construct the lattice
and quenched disorder. The resulting rationing was reproducible without
giving the same traders permanent priority.

For every rule-switch pair, we asserted exact equality of the frame at
$T_{\rm pre}$, the Sell-fraction history through that frame, the edge
coordinates and band widths, and the price history through the quote formed
from the common pre-switch state. For every held-boundary balance-sheet cell we
also asserted equality of the complete Sell-fraction history and final frame
across all nine cash--share interventions. The assertions tested that paired
experiments differed only at the stated intervention.

\subsection{Conservation and limiting cases}

With no intervention, agent-plus-dealer cash and shares are invariants:
\begin{align}
 \sum_i w_i(t)+W_t &= \sum_i w_i(0)+W_0,\\
 \sum_i q_i(t)+Q_t &= \sum_i q_i(0)+Q_0.
\end{align}
Across the production reservoir and finite-size campaigns, the maximum
absolute cash error was $2.53\times10^{-6}$ and the share error was zero. In
the balance-sheet campaign, conservation was checked separately before and
after the intentional intervention jump. Its maximum cash error was
$9.47\times10^{-7}$ and its share error was zero.

Three exact limits supplied regression tests. With $h=\infty$, every
agent-eligible order filled, and the complete path reproduced the earlier
fill-all implementation. With $h=0$, only opposing agent orders cleared,
executed imbalance was zero, the price stayed fixed, and the
endogenous-boundary lattice matched the held-boundary lattice. In the
reservoir campaign, $h=10$ never rejected an order and reproduced the
$h=\infty$ paths exactly. The $h=10$ equality held for the stated endowments
and horizon and was not a general identity between finite and unlimited
dealers.

\subsection{Campaign inventory}

The production inventory contained 30,550 simulations across the four
campaigns (Table~\ref{tab:campaigns}). The reservoir campaign reused one
no-switch control within each $(h,\text{boundary},\text{seed})$ cell, so its
comma-separated value file contained one treated summary row per target
fraction without duplicating the control. The other campaigns reconstructed a
deterministic row order from independently checkpointed cells after threaded
computation. This organization made a resumed threaded run reproduce the same
tables as an uninterrupted run.
