\section{Conclusion}
% ============================================================================

Activating one local response destabilized market paths that had remained
stable under the baseline rule. The paired design established that claim
without comparing separately initialized regimes. Crossings also occurred
under the held boundary, and the crossover sharpened as lattice size
increased. Time-sliced markets showed Sell-rich regions growing and merging
across doses, seeds, and dealer conditions. Together, the intervention,
spatial panels, and finite-size measurements identified a robust spatial
instability while leaving classical nucleation and an equilibrium critical
point unclaimed.

The explicit dealer separated that spatial instability from its financial
expression. Dealer capacity strongly ordered drawdown and rejection but
changed transition probability mainly inside the narrow crossover. Trader
shares set potential sell flow, trader cash supplied matching buy flow, and
dealer resources capped the unmatched flow that reached the quote. Sell-state
dominance, price decline, and dealer exhaustion could therefore move
separately. The unlimited dealer remains a useful mathematical limit, but it
is not an innocuous assumption about execution.
